About

I am a data scientist working on data-efficient deep learning: getting reliable models out of scarce labels, noisy multimodal inputs, and data distributions that shift underneath you. My research spans generative AI and large language models, text mining, and machine learning, with over 15 years of research and development experience and nine years post-PhD.

My work has been funded by the Australian Research Council, government agencies and private industry, and has produced deployed systems as well as publications — including a bias-detection model commercialised in the United States and now used by Fortune 500 companies. Research from my group has been covered in more than 200 national and international media outlets.

I am currently a member of the QUT node of MACSYS, the ARC Centre of Excellence for the Mathematical Analysis of Cellular Systems, where I work on biomedical relation extraction and multi-omics fusion for whole-cell modelling.

Latest. 2026 — four journal articles in press: Neural Networks, Expert Systems with Applications (×2) and Intelligent Systems with Applications. Recent work appeared at ACL 2025.
